How I got to Prince Sultan University

Every good story needs a plot twist. Mine showed up early.

I started at another university, in a major about as far from computers as you can get. But I had a plan, and better yet, a deal: admissions promised that if I reached a GPA of 4.61 out of 5, a seat in the computing college was mine. Guaranteed.

So I got to work, chased that number, and didn't just reach it. I beat it.

Target
4.61
Achieved
4.72
Seats given
0

Then came the twist. There was no seat, just an answer telling me to stay in my original major. Apparently "guaranteed" came with fine print nobody showed me.

So I did the one thing the deal didn't account for: I left. I moved to Prince Sultan University and started over in Information Systems.

Best decision I never planned. It led me to the cybersecurity track, a field built on reading the fine print and never trusting a promise you can't verify. Fitting, really.
